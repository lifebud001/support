Getting Started with LifeBud

LifeBud is a comprehensive fitness tracking app designed to help users create, track, and share their workout journey. The app works around three main workout categories: weightlifting, cardio, and functional training. Users can either choose from pre-built workout plans or generate custom workouts using the AI assistant. When you first open the app, you're greeted with a clean dashboard showing your recent workout history, personal records, and quick access to start a new session. The app includes a complete exercise database with detailed instructions and muscle group targeting, so you always know exactly how to perform each movement.

Workout Session Experience

The workout session experience is where LifeBud really shines. Once you select or create a workout plan, you can start a live session that guides you through each exercise step-by-step. The app displays horizontal scrollable exercise cards that turn green as you complete them, making it easy to track your progress. For weightlifting exercises, you log sets, reps, and weights, while cardio exercises track duration and intensity. There's even a built-in rest timer between sets, and you can add notes or modify exercises on the fly. The session interface is designed to work seamlessly on mobile devices, so you can keep your phone with you throughout your workout.

Monitoring Fitness Progress

Beyond just tracking workouts, LifeBud includes powerful features for monitoring your fitness progress. The personal records section automatically tracks your maximum weights for each exercise, showing when you achieved each milestone and from which workout session. The workout history provides detailed analytics with filtering options by date range, exercise type, and keywords, giving you insights into your training patterns. The app also calculates statistics like total volume lifted, workout frequency, and progress trends over time.

Social Features and Subscription Plans

The social aspect of LifeBud adds motivation and community to your fitness journey. You can share your personal records and achievements with other users through an Instagram-like feed, complete with the ability to post updates about your workouts and celebrate milestones. The app supports both monthly and yearly subscription plans, with the AI-enhanced tier providing personalized workout generation, GPS tracking for outdoor cardio, and advanced training recommendations. Everything is designed to work offline-first, so your workouts are never interrupted, and all data syncs when you're back online.
